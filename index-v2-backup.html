What your site is costing you — Good Vibrations Tattoo & Piercing Boutique —

I put together a free audit of the tweaks I'd make, plus a quick look at how your business shows up when someone searches for what you do. I went through goodvibeztattoo.com the way someone who just searched for a tattoo artist or a piercer in Las Vegas would: land on the page, see who the artists are and what a visit costs, then work out how to reach you. The foundation is strong: seven artist pages with their own Instagram links, a gallery of about 215 pictures that all have descriptions, piercing prices posted in plain numbers, a sitemap and business markup that match what's on the page, and a 4.9 Google rating shown on the home and Reviews pages. The leaks are in the details: the home page gives two different sets of opening hours, it says texting is the fastest way to reach you but every button opens the phone dialer, a link in your Terms page lands on a “Page Not Found” screen, the Reviews page shows a system message to visitors, and every page names the home page as its preferred address.

Checked October 18, 2026

What I found

01

The home page gives two different sets of opening hours.

Your Contact section says “Sunday - Tuesday: 10:30 AM - 6:30 PM” and “Wednesday - Saturday: 10:30 AM - 9:00 PM”, and so do your hero area, your footer, the markup behind the page and your Yelp listing. Further down the home page, the strip under “Book with any of our talented artists” says “Open 7 Days a Week”, “Mon-Tue: 10:30AM-6:30PM” and “Wed-Sun: 10:30AM-10:00PM”. That strip puts Sunday closing at 10:00 PM instead of 6:30 PM and Wednesday to Saturday at 10:00 PM instead of 9:00 PM. A visitor who reads the lower strip could arrive after you've closed. I read both on October 18, 2026, and I haven't called to ask which is right.

02

The page says texting is the fastest way to reach you, but every button opens the phone dialer.

Your home page says “Text preferred”, “Texting preferred for quick responses” and “Text (725) 529-8423 for fastest response”. The buttons say “TEXT OR CALL TO BOOK”, “TEXT/CALL FOR CONSULTATION”, “TEXT/CALL TO BOOK” and “TEXT/CALL TO BEGIN”, and all of them link to tel:7255298423, which starts a phone call on a phone. I counted 8 of those links on the home page and 40 across your 16 pages, and none of them is a text-message link. A visitor who wants to text has to copy the number into their messages app. I read the links in the page code and haven't tapped them on a phone.

03

A link in your Terms of Service page goes to a “Page Not Found” screen.

Under Aftercare, the Terms of Service page has a link, “View Complete Aftercare Instructions →”, that goes to goodvibeztattoo.com/aftercare. That address shows “404 Page Not Found” and “Did you forget to add the page to the router?”, which reads like a builder's message, not yours. Your aftercare guide does exist: the AFTERCARE item in your menu opens it at /news. The same screen appears for any address that doesn't exist, such as /services/, and the server answers those with a normal “page found” code (200), so Google can't tell they're missing. I opened these on October 18, 2026.

04

The Reviews page shows visitors a system message.

Each time the Reviews page loads, a box appears at the bottom right reading “Reviews Updated: Successfully synced 5 most helpful reviews from 1015 total Google reviews.” It was still on screen several seconds after the page loaded. The same page has a “Refresh Reviews” button that any visitor can press. Both read like tools for whoever manages the site, shown on a page meant to build trust. I loaded the page in a desktop browser and haven't pressed the button.

05

Every page tells Google its preferred address is the home page.

I loaded all 16 pages in your sitemap. On every one, including /artist/crab, /gallery, /piercings and /reviews, the canonical tag (the line that names a page's preferred address) is https://goodvibeztattoo.com/. Separately, the file your server sends for any address is the same one: the home page's title and an empty page body. The words, and each page's own title and description, appear only after the browser runs JavaScript. Google runs JavaScript, but not every crawler or assistant does. Both www.goodvibeztattoo.com and goodvibeztattoo.com load without one redirecting to the other. I can't tell how Google treats any of this; I'm only reporting what the pages say.

06

Five icons on the home page draw as nothing, and five icon-only links have no name.

On all 16 pages your browser logs an error because the Font Awesome script (kit.fontawesome.com/a076d05399.js) is blocked. On the home page, five icons come out at zero size: the clock, phone and pin above the hours, phone number and address in the “Book with any of our talented artists” strip (so that row shows text with no icons), and the small icons on “Write a Google Review” and “View Full Gallery”. Lighthouse also flagged five links with no discernible name: the Facebook and Instagram icons in two places, and the round call button that floats on the home page. A screen reader announces those as just “link”. I haven't tested with a screen reader.

What's working

Your artist pages are a real strength: seven artists and piercers each with their own page, specialties, years of experience and a link to their own Instagram. The gallery holds about 215 pictures and every one has a description. Your Piercings page posts prices in plain numbers, from $55 for an earlobe to $150, with titanium jewelry and aftercare included. Your address, phone and email are on the page, a tap-to-call link is always in the menu, and a message sent through your contact form this morning reached you. The home page carries business markup with your address, phone, hours, founders and Facebook and Instagram links, your sitemap lists 16 pages that all load, and every page has its own title and description. The home and Reviews pages show 4.9 from 1,015 Google reviews. Lighthouse lab scores on a simulated phone: performance 73 and 74, accessibility 79, best practices 75, SEO 100; layout shift 0 (Google treats 0.1 or less as good).

    Your robots.txt allows GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-Web, Google-Extended, anthropic-ai and cohere-ai by name, and has a Sitemap line.

  • Found
    Sitemap

    /sitemap.xml lists 16 pages. All 16 load.

  • Found
    Business markup

    The home page has TattooParlor markup with your name, address, phone, email, hours, founders and links to Facebook and Instagram. The hours in it match your contact block.

  • Found
    llms.txt

    /llms.txt is a plain-text summary of the business with its address, hours, services and artists.

  • Gap
    The llms.txt price doesn't match your Piercings page

    /llms.txt says “Pricing starts at $65”, and your Piercings page lists “1 for $55 / 2 for $85” for earlobes.

  • Gap
    Page text isn't in the first file the server sends

    For every address the server sends the home page's title and an empty body, and the canonical tag names the home page. The page's own words appear after JavaScript runs.

  • Gap

    On the home page Lighthouse estimates 564 KiB could be saved by sending pictures at the size they're shown, and 274 KiB with newer picture formats. First content showed at 3.4 seconds and the largest item at 4.5 seconds in both runs, on a lab simulation of a slow phone.

  • Gap
    Two web addresses serve the same site

    https://www.goodvibeztattoo.com/ and https://goodvibeztattoo.com/ both return the page, with no redirect from one to the other.

  • How many people search for these terms or how many visit your site.

What I'd fix first

Make the lower strip on the home page match your real hours, or fix the hours everywhere if the strip is right. Point the call buttons that say “text” at a text-message link, or add one beside them, so anyone who taps to text lands in their messages. Fix or remove the Aftercare link in the Terms page, and make unknown addresses return a real “not found” page. Hide the “Reviews Updated” message and the Refresh button from visitors. Give each page its own canonical address and send the same page text in the first file. Fix the Font Awesome script so the icons draw, and give the icon links names. Send pictures at the size they're shown. I can't promise where any of it will place you.
